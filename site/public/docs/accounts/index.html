Prop accounts and firm rules

Roundturn checks each evaluation and funded account against its firm's rules, from the trades you import: how far you are from a breach and from the goal, and when a payout is ready.

For your information

Roundturn works the rules out from your closed trades, so its numbers may differ from your firm's own. The firm's numbers are the ones that count. A rule that needs open-trade equity, such as an intraday trailing drawdown, says "Not verifiable from imports" rather than pass.

An account's settings

Each account in Settings › Prop accounts has these tabs:

  • Details: name, firm, stage, size, starting balance and purchase date.
  • Fees: the commission per contract, round trip, for each contract you trade. Every trade's fees come from here.
  • Rules: a simple profit target, max drawdown, trailing type and daily loss limit, if you track your own.
  • Firm rules: your firm's rule set, picked and verified (below).
  • Payouts: each payout you received, gross, with your split and the net.
  • Prop spend: what the account cost you: fees, resets, data.

Set the firm's rules

Open the account's Firm rules tab and find your firm, program, size and phase. Only the versions in force on your purchase date are offered, and the latest is marked Suggested. Check every value against your firm's own page, then Verify. If your program is not listed, start blank and type the rules in.

The Accounts tab

The Accounts tab lists every active account against its verified rules. It changes nothing: each account's ⋯ menu leads to its settings.

  • Payout shows qualifying days, the consistency rule and the buffer still to build, or what is ready to withdraw.
  • Evaluation passed? The Accounts tab offers to set up your funded account.
  • Passed, failed or closed accounts can be archived from their settings. Archived accounts are left out of the numbers unless you turn on Show archived.
  • Prop business, in Settings › Prop accounts, totals your evaluation pass rate, your prop spend, your payouts and the difference.
