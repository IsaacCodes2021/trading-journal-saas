Import from the Round Turn Monitor

The Round Turn Monitor is an add-on for NinjaTrader 8. After every round turn it adds a row to its log, for every account NinjaTrader tracks, and its Export button saves the same rows for one day. Roundturn reads either, and a round turn already imported is never added twice.

The file
An Export file, or RoundTurnLog.csv
In Roundturn
NinjaTrader: Round Turn Monitor log
Times
Your CSV time zone setting

  1. In NinjaTrader

    Click Export, or use the log

    After the session, click Export in the Round Turn Monitor window. It saves the trading day's round turns, for one account or all of them, in the same columns as the log. Or skip it: the add-on writes every round turn to its log by itself, in Documents › NinjaTrader 8 › RoundTurnLog.

  2. In Roundturn

    Choose NinjaTrader: Round Turn Monitor log

    On the Import page, choose the Roundturn account the trades belong to, then Choose files. Roundturn asks where the trades are from.

  3. On your computer

    Choose the Export file, or the log

    Choose the file Export saved. Or go to Documents › NinjaTrader 8 › RoundTurnLog and choose RoundTurnLog.csv.

    If RoundTurnLog_overflow.csv is there too, choose both: hold Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) and click each. The add-on writes to the overflow file while the log is open in Excel, so some round turns are only in that file.

  4. In Roundturn

    Pick the NinjaTrader account, check, and import

    One import takes one NinjaTrader account. An Export file of one account needs no choice. The log, or an Export of all accounts, holds every account NinjaTrader tracks, Sim101 included: the preview picks the account this Roundturn account already holds, or the only real one. Practice accounts (Sim101, Playback, Backtest) are never picked for you.

Good to know

  • Choose the same log every time, or each day's Export file. Round turns already imported are left alone, and only the new ones are added. A round turn in both an Export file and the log counts once.
  • Several NinjaTrader accounts? Import the log once for each, each into its own Roundturn account. Copied trades can go into several accounts.
  • Times are read in your CSV time zone setting (in Roundturn's Settings), because the log writes NinjaTrader's clock without a time zone.
  • Commissions come from each account's own commission setting, as for every import, not from the log. Round turns logged before the add-on's rate was set show $0.00 in the log; Roundturn charges them the same as the rest.

If the preview says

"This log has round turns from 2 accounts. Choose which one to import into this account."
More than one real account is in the log and Roundturn cannot tell which one belongs here. Pick it in the list.
"A round turn is missing its other half."
Part of the log was left out, usually the overflow file. Choose the whole log again, with RoundTurnLog_overflow.csv if it is there.
"This Export file is from an older version of the Round Turn Monitor, without the contract or commission."
Exports made before the add-on was updated cannot be read safely: NQ and MNQ trade at the same price. Update the add-on and export again, or choose RoundTurnLog.csv, which has the same round turns.
"This is the Round Turn Monitor's old log"
That is the 19-column log of an earlier version of the add-on. Choose RoundTurnLog.csv, which the add-on writes now.
