Getting started

From sign-up to your first reviewed session in four steps. When you first sign in, a short setup walks you through the same steps; this page is here for when you want them again.

  1. Settings › Preferences

    Check your time zones

    Roundturn needs to know two clocks: the one you want times shown in, and the one your files are written in. If the second is wrong, trades land hours off and on the wrong trading day.

  2. Settings › Prop accounts

    Add your accounts

    Add each evaluation and funded account with + Add account: its name, firm, stage, size, starting balance and the day you bought it. The purchase date matters: it decides which version of the firm's rules applies. What it cost can be added now or later.

  3. The account's Fees tab

    Set each account's fees

    Roundturn charges each trade the account's own commission rate for the contract, per contract, round trip. With no rate set, your net P&L is too high; the import preview tells you when the file's commissions differ and offers to use its rate. See Prop accounts and rules.

  4. Import

    Import your trades

    Choose the account, then Choose files, and pick your platform. Nothing is saved until you confirm the preview. Each platform has its guide: Tradovate, TradeSea, Round Turn Monitor.

Then

  • The Dashboard and Reports show your results: how the numbers work.
  • Open any trade to add a note, tags and screenshots: your journal.
  • The Accounts tab checks each account against its firm's rules once you have set them: prop accounts and rules.
