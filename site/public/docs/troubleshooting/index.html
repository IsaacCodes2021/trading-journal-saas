When something goes wrong

The usual causes, and what to check first.

The numbers differ from Tradovate's
Most often a different clock: check the two time zones in Settings › Preferences. Then the fees: Roundturn charges each account's own commission setting.
The numbers differ from TradeSea's
Most often a missing page of the Orders export, or a different commission rate. The import preview checks both: see the TradeSea guide.
The numbers differ from the Round Turn Monitor's
The add-on's net uses its own commission rate, which may have been $0.00 for round turns logged before it was set. Roundturn charges every trade the account's fee setting. See the Round Turn Monitor guide.
The numbers differ from another platform's statement
The import preview compares the chatbot's file with the net P&L you typed from your statement: a difference there means a trade is missing, doubled or wrong in the file. After the import, check the fees: Roundturn charges the account's own commission setting, not the file's fees. See the guide for any other platform.
A trade is on the wrong day
Check when your trading day starts, and the zone your files are written in, both in Settings › Preferences. Changing them moves your existing trades to match.
A page says "The server had a problem" and gives a reference
Quote the reference when you write to us: it finds the exact request.
Pictures will not load or save, and a message says they are not working right now
A temporary problem on our side. Try again in a minute.

Writing to us

Write to hello@roundturn.app from the address on your account. Never send a password or a one-time code: we will never ask for one.

A question this does not answer?

Write to hello@roundturn.app. We read every message.
