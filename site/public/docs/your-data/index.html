Docs / Your data

Your data

Everything you keep in Roundturn is yours: you can take it all with you, or delete it.

Both are in Settings › Data & privacy.

Who can see it

Only you. Roundturn's operator tools show an account's plan and counts, never a trade, a note or a screenshot, and they cannot sign in as you. The privacy policy says what is stored and where.

Your sign-ins

Settings › Security shows where you are signed in and your recent activity, and lets you sign a place out.

A question this does not answer?

Write to hello@roundturn.app. We read every message.
