Your journal

Open any trade from the Trades list or the Calendar to see its fills and write about it: a note, tags and screenshots. The Calendar and Reports then show the patterns.

A trade's page

  • Notes save by themselves a moment after you stop typing. Insert template fills in a note outline.
  • The fill pairs show each entry with the exit that closed it, and the trade's gross, fees and net.

Tags and screenshots

  • Screenshots can be PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 10 MB each, and a trade can hold 20. There is also a limit on the space your pictures take in all; the page tells you when you reach it. Other kinds of file, including SVG, are refused on purpose.

The Calendar and Reports

  • The Calendar shows each day's result, and has a place to write a note for the day, with or without trades.
  • Reports break your trades down by contract, weekday, time of day, hold time, tag and account. A trade with several tags is counted under each of them.

The share card

Share, on a trade's page, makes an image of the trade to save or post. You can add one of its screenshots, cropped to fit, and sign it with your username (Settings › Preferences). Points only hides the dollars, quantity and fees.
