Who it is for

Prop-firm futures traders

Several accounts across several firms: evaluations, funded accounts, their fees and their payouts, in one place.

Tradovate users

Traders who review

What it does

Import with a preview

Upload the Tradovate Performance CSV and see what will be added before anything is saved. Import the same file twice and nothing doubles. Undo any import.

A dashboard that adds up

Net P&L after fees, win rate, profit factor, expectancy, maximum drawdown, an equity curve and daily results.

Every trade, with your notes

Filter and search the trade log. Add notes, tags for setup, mistake and emotion, and screenshots to any trade.

Calendar and reports

A day-by-day calendar with a note for each day. Results by contract, weekday, hour, hold time, tag and account.

Prop accounts

Track evaluations and funded accounts with their fees, their rules and a ledger of costs and payouts.

Your data stays yours

Export everything as a ZIP at any time. Delete your account and its data when you choose.

Where it falls short today

Status: in development. We would rather tell you now.

Tradovate only

Trades arrive from the Performance CSV you export and upload. There is no automatic sync yet.

Firm rules are informational

Rule progress may differ from your firm's official numbers. The firm's numbers are the ones that count.

No live rule tracking

Roundturn does not watch your accounts as you trade or warn you before a rule is hit.

Desktop first

It works on a phone, but it is designed for a larger screen.

Rough edges

Expect some while the beta runs. Support is by email, on a best-effort basis.

Not a trading tool

It does not place or route trades, and it is not investment advice.
