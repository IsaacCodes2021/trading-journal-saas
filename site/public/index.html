Where it falls short today

Roundturn is young, and these are its limits today. We would rather tell you now.

Tradovate only

Trades arrive from the Performance CSV you export and upload. There is no automatic sync yet.

Firm rules are informational

Rule progress may differ from your firm's official numbers. The firm's numbers are the ones that count.

No live rule tracking

Roundturn does not watch your accounts as you trade or warn you before a rule is hit.

Desktop first

It works on a phone, but it is designed for a larger screen.

Rough edges

Expect some as Roundturn keeps improving. Support is by email, on a best-effort basis.

Not a trading tool

It does not place or route trades, and it is not investment advice.
