Roundturn is a journal and analytics tool for futures traders. Upload the trade history you export from your own platform and see what it says: profit and loss, win rate, drawdown, and results by day and by instrument. Add notes and screenshots to any trade.

What it does

Import your own history

Upload the Performance export from your Tradovate account. Re-importing the same rows never duplicates them, and any import can be undone.

One trade, one round turn

A round turn is one complete trade, from the entry to the exit. Roundturn combines the scale-ins and scale-outs in your export into single trades, so the numbers match how you actually traded.

Statistics you can check

Net profit and loss after your fees, win rate, drawdown, and results by day and by instrument. Money is computed exactly, not rounded along the way.

Notes, tags and screenshots

Write down what you saw and why you took the trade, tag your setups and mistakes, and attach a chart image to any trade.

Prop account tracking

If you trade accounts from a prop firm, record their rules and fees to follow your own progress. It is for your tracking only and may differ from the firm's official numbers.

Your data stays yours

Roundturn never asks for broker passwords or keys, and each user's data is separated from everyone else's at the database level.

What it is not

  • It does not place or route trades, and it does not connect to your broker or exchange account.
  • It gives no investment advice, trade signals, recommendations or price predictions.
  • It does not hold, move or manage anyone's money.
  • It is not a proprietary trading firm and does not sell funded accounts or evaluations.

More on the about page, including the risk disclosure.

Roundturn is software for reviewing your own trading history. It is not investment advice. Trading futures involves substantial risk of loss, and past results do not predict future results.
