The desk

What it does

Every trade built from your export, with the numbers a review needs and the mistake tags that explain them.

Import with a preview

Upload the Tradovate Performance CSV and see what will be added before anything is saved. Import the same file twice and nothing doubles. Undo any import.

A dashboard that adds up

Net P&L after fees, win rate, profit factor, expectancy, maximum drawdown, an equity curve and daily results.

Every trade, with your notes

Filter and search the trade log. Add notes, tags for setup, mistake and emotion, and screenshots to any trade.

Calendar and reports

A day-by-day calendar with a note for each day. Results by contract, weekday, hour, hold time, tag and account.

Prop accounts

Track evaluations and funded accounts with their fees, their rules and a ledger of costs and payouts.

Your data stays yours

Export everything as a ZIP at any time. Delete your account and its data when you choose.
