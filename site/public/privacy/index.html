About Privacy

Privacy

This page covers the waitlist only, and it is a draft. A full privacy policy will be written, and reviewed by a professional, before the app opens to users. Last updated 2026-10-03.

What is collected

If you join the waitlist, Roundturn stores two things: your email address and the date you joined. Nothing else. It does not store your IP address, your browser details or where you came from.

Why

To send you one email when your invitation to the beta is ready. The list is not sold, rented or shared, and it is not used for anything else.

What this site does not do

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and loads nothing from other websites.

Where it is stored

This site and its waitlist run on Cloudflare. Your address is kept in a database there, and Cloudflare, like any web host, can see technical request data such as IP addresses while it serves the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

How long

Until your invitation has been sent or you ask for the address to be removed, whichever comes first.

Your choices

To see what is stored about you, or to have it deleted, email hello@roundturn.app from the address in question. It will be deleted and you will get a short confirmation.
