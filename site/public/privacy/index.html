Privacy

This page covers the beta sign-up form only, and it is a draft. A full privacy policy will be written, and reviewed by a professional, before the app opens to users. Last updated 2026-10-10.

What is collected

If you join the beta, Roundturn stores your first name, your email address, the prop firm you named, and when you joined. It also keeps a record of the sign-up code it made for you (as a one-way hash, so the code itself cannot be read back) and whether the email with the code was sent. It does not store your IP address, your browser details or where you came from.

Why

To email you your sign-up code, and to run the beta: to know who has a code and whose 30 days are running. You may get one more email when your access ends. The list is not sold, rented or shared, and it is not used for anything else.

What this site does not do

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and loads nothing from other websites.

Where it is stored

This site runs on Cloudflare. The sign-up form and its records are on Roundturn's application servers, which run on Render in the United States, with the database there. The email with your code is sent through Resend, an email delivery service, so Resend handles your address and first name to deliver it. Like any web host, these services can see technical request data such as IP addresses while they serve a page or deliver a message. See the privacy policies of Cloudflare, Render and Resend.

How long

Until you ask for it to be removed. Nothing is deleted automatically yet; a fixed period will be set, and written here, before the beta opens widely.

Your choices

To see what is stored about you, or to have it deleted, email hello@roundturn.app from the address in question. It will be deleted and you will get a short confirmation.
