Billing and refunds

This policy is a draft and not legal advice: it will be reviewed by a professional before paid plans open. Last updated 2026-10-14.

Where things stand

Paid plans are not open yet. Roundturn is in a free, invite-only beta, and nobody is being charged. This page says what will apply when paid plans open. It is part of the Terms of Service.

Price

Roundturn Pro: USD 15 per month. There is one paid plan and no free plan. Prices are in US dollars, and applicable taxes are added at checkout.

The 7-day trial

New users get a 7-day trial of the full app. After the trial, a paid subscription is needed to keep using it. The trial is how you decide whether Roundturn is worth paying for, before any payment is taken.

How billing works

  • A subscription renews every month, on the same day, until you cancel.
  • Payments are taken by a payment provider that acts as the seller of record. We plan to use Lemon Squeezy. Roundturn never sees or stores your card number.
  • If a renewal payment fails, the provider may try again. If it still fails, your access becomes read-only: you keep your data and can look at it and download it.
  • If we change the price, we will tell you before it applies to your next renewal, and you can cancel before then.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, in the app (Settings, Plan) or by emailing hello@roundturn.app. Cancelling stops the next renewal. Your access continues to the end of the month you have already paid for, and then your journal becomes read-only. Deleting your account also ends your subscription, so you are not charged again.

No refunds

We do not issue refunds. Payments are final and non-refundable, including for a partial month, for unused time after you cancel, and if you forget to cancel before a renewal. Please use the 7-day trial and cancel before it ends if Roundturn is not for you.

Two things this does not change:

  • Billing mistakes. If you were charged twice, or charged after you cancelled, email us at hello@roundturn.app with the details. We will look into it and put right any charge that was our error.
  • Your legal rights. Where the law of your country gives you a right to a refund or to withdraw from a purchase, and that right cannot be waived, it still applies. This policy does not take it away.

If you have a problem with a charge, please write to us first. We would rather fix it with you directly.
