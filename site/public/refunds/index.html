Billing and refunds

Last updated 2026-10-24.

How it works

Roundturn Pro is a paid subscription with a 7-day free trial. This page says how billing, cancelling and refunds work. It is part of the Terms of Service.

Price

Roundturn Pro: USD 15 per month. There is one paid plan and no free plan. Prices are in US dollars, and applicable taxes are added at checkout.

The 7-day trial

New users get a 7-day trial of the full app. After the trial, a paid subscription is needed to keep using it. The trial is how you decide whether Roundturn is worth paying for, before any payment is taken.

How billing works

  • A subscription renews every month, on the same day, at USD 15 plus any applicable tax, until you cancel.
  • Payments are taken by Stripe through Link. Sold through Link, LLC, an affiliate of Stripe, is the merchant of record: it charges your payment method, collects sales tax or VAT where it applies, and sends your receipt. The charge shows on your card statement starting with "link.com*". Roundturn never sees or stores your card number.
  • If a renewal payment fails, Stripe may try again. If it still fails, your access becomes read-only: you keep your data and can look at it and download it.
  • If we change the price, we will tell you before it applies to your next renewal, and you can cancel before then.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, in the app (Settings, Plan) or by emailing hello@roundturn.app. Cancelling stops the next renewal. Your access continues to the end of the month you have already paid for, and then your journal becomes read-only. Deleting your account also ends your subscription, so you are not charged again. You can also manage or cancel the subscription, and update your payment method, at link.com; your receipt from Link shows how.

Refunds

We do not issue refunds. Payments are final and non-refundable by Roundturn, including for a partial month, for unused time after you cancel, and if you forget to cancel before a renewal. Please use the 7-day trial and cancel before it ends if Roundturn is not for you.

Three things this does not change:

  • Billing mistakes. If you were charged twice, or charged after you cancelled, email us at hello@roundturn.app with the details. We will look into it and put right any charge that was our error.
  • Your legal rights. Where the law of your country gives you a right to a refund or to withdraw from a purchase, such as the cooling-off period some countries give consumers, and that right cannot be waived, it still applies. This policy does not take it away.
  • Link's refund policy. Link is the merchant of record, so it can refund a payment even where we do not. You can ask Link for a refund within 60 days of a payment if you did not receive what you paid for, if it was significantly not as described or unfit for its purpose, if it was damaged or corrupted, or if the payment was made without your permission or duplicated in error. Link does not review requests for a change of mind, or for a subscription you did not cancel before its billing date. Link decides under its refund policy, may contact us about your request, and where its policy is more generous than ours, its policy applies.

If you have a problem with a charge, please write to us first. We would rather fix it with you directly.
